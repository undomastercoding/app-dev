# My Favorite Movie: The Hunger Games

![The Hunger Games](https://www.themoviedb.org/t/p/original/1ewwtjqAcwZ5abr2X7coGN0UwaI.jpg)

**Genre:** Action, Science Fiction, Adventure

**Main Characters:** Katniss Everdeen, Peeta Mellark, Gale Hawthorne

**Why I Like It:**
I like watching action and adventure movies. The Hunger Games catch my eyes because it has intense battles and full of exciting moments. Also I like Katniss's determination and courage that inspire me to never give up, even when facing up and downs challenges.
